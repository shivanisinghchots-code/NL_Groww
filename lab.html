MVP · Groww SIP case study

Take a break, don't break up.

When a first-time investor taps Skip, Change or Cancel, the assistant asks what's going on, in their own words and language, then shows options that keep the SIP alive, each with the official Groww Help Centre rule behind it. The choice stays theirs.

A case-study prototype, not affiliated with Groww and not investment advice. It never suggests funds, products or market views, and nothing here changes a real account. Don't enter real personal details; anything that looks like a PAN, phone, email, UPI ID or account number is removed before the AI sees it.

How it works

The AI listens; the rules decide.

The model only reads the message and writes a short, kind reply. Every option, date and consequence comes from code built on Groww's own published rules, so the model can't invent a rule or promise something the app won't do.

  1. CodeClean the inputRemove PAN, phone, email, UPI and account numbers. Cap at 1,000 characters.
  2. AIRead the messageIntent, break length, language (English, Hindi, Hinglish), hardship, and a reply of 45 words or fewer.
  3. CodeGate the replyFormat, no advice, no fear or urgency, no numbers, no claims about what you can do, real citations, same language, declines advice requests.
  4. CodeBuild the planOptions and consequences from rules R1–R13: the 3-in-a-row cancel, 2-day skip notice, 7-day cancel block.
  5. FallbackNever stuckGate fails: a safe default reply. AI down or rate-limited: a rule-only reading. The options and the original action are always shown.

Known limits

  • The options and rule text are in English; only the AI's reply follows the user's language.
  • The SIP state (missed in a row, days to next) is set by the demo controls. In the app it would come from the SIP record.
  • Rules were copied from the Groww Help Centre on 29 Oct 2026. If Groww changes them, lib/break.js must be updated and the evals re-run.
  • Small sample: 32 golden and 12 held-out cases, written by the team, not real user messages.
  • Free AI plan limits: if the daily limit is reached, the assistant switches to the rule-only reading and says so.
